# Inverted Ocean Desert: video prompt (audited)

## Video prompt
Photorealistic cinematic single continuous shot, 8 seconds, 16:9, 24fps.

An endless golden desert beneath an impossible upside-down ocean that fills the entire sky like a vast turquoise ceiling. The ocean's underside ripples slowly and no water ever falls. A low sun behind the far horizon shines through the water, casting drifting, rippling caustic light across the dunes and lighting fine airborne sand in warm amber volumetric rays.

Subject: a lone traveller in weathered charcoal robes, barefoot, seen from behind, standing on the crest of a tall dune facing the horizon. The robes ripple naturally in the wind. The face is never visible. Figure and clothing stay identical throughout.

Camera: wide anamorphic lens, large-format look, natural motion blur. Begins low and close behind the traveller, then makes one smooth continuous move, tilting upward while slowly rising and pulling back.

Timeline:
- 0-2s: Low behind the traveller. Wind in the robes, drifting sand, caustics sliding over the dune, the ocean filling the upper frame.
- 2-4s: Camera tilts up. A gigantic humpback whale, with long pectoral flippers and throat grooves visible from below, emerges from deep blue darkness at upper left and swims slowly left to right with slow, anatomically accurate tail and flipper motion.
- 4-6s: The whale glides directly overhead. Its vast shadow sweeps across the dunes and the traveller, who slowly lifts their head in silent astonishment.
- 6-8s: A few water droplets lift from the sand toward the ocean, glinting. The camera settles on a wide final frame: a tiny traveller, endless dunes, the whale and ocean towering overhead. Hold.

Look: deep turquoise and warm amber grade, subtle anamorphic flare, atmospheric haze, intricate sand texture, fine film grain, monumental scale, dreamlike mood.

## Audio prompt
Deep atmospheric sub-bass rumble, soft desert wind, faint sand grains brushing fabric. Distant low whale calls from 2-6s. A gentle ethereal string swell as the whale passes overhead (4-6s). Ends on a sustained mysterious ambient tone. Immersive spatial audio, smooth transitions, instrumental only.

## Negative prompt
cartoon, illustration, CGI look, low-resolution textures, malformed whale anatomy, duplicated or extra whales, distorted human anatomy, visible face, character morphing, changing clothing, ocean collapsing, water falling, rain, flooding, unstable horizon, camera jitter, abrupt zoom, cuts, flickering, texture swimming, excessive lens flare, oversaturation, inconsistent shadows, text, subtitles, logos, watermarks, speech, dialogue, choir, vocals, lyrics
