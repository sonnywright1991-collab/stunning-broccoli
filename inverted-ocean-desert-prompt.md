# Inverted Ocean Desert: video prompt (audited, v2)

## Single-box prompt (video + audio)
Use this when the tool has one prompt box. Negatives are phrased as positive
constraints, so nothing unwanted gets named.

Photorealistic cinematic single continuous shot, 8 seconds, 16:9, 24fps.

An endless golden desert beneath an impossible upside-down ocean that fills the entire sky like a vast turquoise ceiling. The ocean's underside ripples slowly and stays perfectly suspended. It stretches to the horizon, where a thin band of warm amber haze separates water from sand. The sun sits high above, beyond the water, glowing softly through it. Sunlight filters down through the ocean in turquoise-tinted shafts, throwing slowly moving caustic patterns across the warm golden dunes and lighting fine drifting sand.

Subject: a lone traveller in weathered charcoal robes, barefoot, seen only from behind, standing still on the crest of a tall dune facing the horizon. The robes ripple naturally in the wind. Their face stays hidden. Figure and clothing stay identical throughout.

Camera: wide anamorphic lens, large-format look, natural motion blur. Starts low and close behind the traveller, then makes one slow, smooth, continuous move, rising, pulling back and tilting upward, keeping the traveller in frame as they shrink into the lower third.

Timeline:
0-2s: Low behind the traveller, a dark figure against the glowing water. Wind in the robes, drifting sand, caustics sliding over the dune.
2-4s: As the camera tilts up, a gigantic humpback whale emerges from the hazy blue distance at upper left and swims slowly left to right. Seen from below: pale throat grooves, long white pectoral flippers, slow up-and-down strokes of its tail flukes.
4-6s: The whale glides overhead. Its soft-edged shadow sweeps across the dunes and passes over the traveller, who slowly tilts their head back to look up, otherwise motionless. Glinting water droplets begin to lift from the sand around their feet.
6-8s: The droplets keep rising toward the ocean as a sparkling trail. The camera eases to a stop on the wide final frame: a tiny traveller in the lower third, endless dunes, the whale and ocean towering overhead. Hold.

Look: deep turquoise and warm amber grade, subtle horizontal anamorphic flare from the bright water, atmospheric haze, intricate sand texture, fine film grain, monumental scale, dreamlike mood.

Audio: deep sub-bass rumble, soft desert wind, faint sand grains brushing fabric. Distant low whale calls from 2-6s. A gentle ethereal string swell as the whale passes overhead (4-6s). Ends on a sustained mysterious ambient tone. Immersive spatial audio, smooth transitions, instrumental only.

## Negative prompt
Only for a tool with a separate negative-prompt field. Do not paste it into the main prompt box.

cartoon, illustration, CGI look, low-resolution textures, malformed whale anatomy, duplicated or extra whales, distorted human anatomy, visible face, character morphing, changing clothing, ocean collapsing, water falling, rain, flooding, unstable horizon, camera jitter, abrupt zoom, cuts, flickering, texture swimming, excessive lens flare, oversaturation, inconsistent shadows, text, subtitles, logos, watermarks, speech, dialogue, choir, vocals, lyrics

## Compact prompt (under 1,000 characters)
For tools with a short prompt limit. Video only; add audio in post.

Photorealistic single continuous shot. An endless golden desert beneath an upside-down ocean filling the whole sky; its turquoise underside ripples slowly, perfectly suspended. High sunlight filters down through the water, casting moving caustics on the dunes. A lone barefoot traveller in charcoal robes stands on a dune crest, seen from behind. The camera starts low behind them, then slowly rises, pulls back and tilts up. A giant humpback whale glides left to right through the ocean overhead with slow up-and-down fluke strokes; its soft shadow sweeps over the traveller, who tilts their head back to look up. Glinting droplets rise from the sand toward the ocean. Ends wide: tiny figure, vast dunes, whale overhead. Wide anamorphic lens, turquoise and amber grade, film grain, dreamlike scale.
