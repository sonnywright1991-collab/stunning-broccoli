Sonny's World

Sonny's World is a web-collage of text, pictures and noise assembled from one house in Wagga Wagga, in the manner of the old GeoCities homepages (1994–2009). It is a love letter to the Internet of old, and a tribute to Cameron's World.

Collected and assembled by Sonny. Built with Claude. Music: one cheerful looping MIDI-style tune, synthesised live in your browser. Everything here is served from this page; there is no other archive.
